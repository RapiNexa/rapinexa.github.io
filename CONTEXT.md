# RapiNexa Company Profile

The public, single-page company profile of RapiNexa: it advertises RapiNexa's Service lines to Indonesian small businesses and showcases past Works to earn trust, funnelling visitors into a WhatsApp conversation.

## Language

**RapiNexa**:
The web developer team that owns this site and sells the Service lines. Speaks as a team, never as an individual.
_Avoid_: Aldila, the portfolio owner, "saya"

**Service line**:
One of RapiNexa's four kinds of work, in this order: Pembuatan Website, Aplikasi Web Bisnis, Maintenance Website, Perbaikan & Fitur. The company-profile deck is the source of truth for their names, scope, and prices.
_Avoid_: Service, skill, product, feature

**Website tier**:
A priced level of the Pembuatan Website line: Landing Page, Company Profile All-In, or Custom Web Professional.
_Avoid_: Package, plan

**Maintenance package**:
A monthly care plan for an existing website or application: Professional Care or Custom Application Care.
_Avoid_: Subscription, retainer, Bundle

**Starting price**:
The lowest price at which a Website tier or Maintenance package is offered, shown as "mulai dari Rp…". Indicative, not a quote. Aplikasi Web Bisnis and Perbaikan & Fitur have no Starting price; they are priced per scope after consultation.
_Avoid_: Fixed price, tariff

**Work**:
Everything RapiNexa delivered for one Client, shown as proof of capability and described by what it achieved — without periods, job roles, or departments. One Work per Client.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, experience, job, project

**Client**:
The business a Work was delivered for (e.g. Djampi Jawi, Ella Skin Care).
_Avoid_: Company, employer

**Deliverable**:
A single system delivered within a Work (e.g. "POS Kasir", "Panel Admin").
_Avoid_: App, sub-project

**Platform**:
A third-party technology or service RapiNexa can build on or integrate into its work (e.g. OpenAI, AWS, Laravel, Midtrans). Shown as a logo only.
_Avoid_: Tech stack, skill, partner

**CTA**:
Any call-to-action on the site; every CTA opens a WhatsApp chat with RapiNexa, optionally pre-filled with the Website tier, Maintenance package, or Service line of interest.
_Avoid_: Contact form, hire me
