# RapiNexa Company Profile

The public, single-page company profile of RapiNexa: it advertises RapiNexa's Services to Indonesian small businesses and showcases past Works to earn trust, funnelling visitors into a WhatsApp conversation.

## Language

**RapiNexa**:
The software developer team that owns this site and sells the Services. Speaks as a team, never as an individual.
_Avoid_: Aldila, the portfolio owner, "saya"

**Service**:
A productized offering RapiNexa sells at a published starting price (e.g. POS Kasir Standard, Video Shorts/Reels).
_Avoid_: Skill, product, feature

**Starting price**:
The lowest price at which a Service is offered, shown as "mulai dari Rp…". Indicative, not a quote.
_Avoid_: Fixed price, tariff

**Bundle**:
A named combination of several Services sold at one price (currently "Paket Digital UMKM").
_Avoid_: Package deal, plan

**Work**:
Everything RapiNexa delivered for one Client, shown as proof of capability and described by what it achieved — without periods, job roles, or departments. One Work per Client.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, experience, job, project

**Client**:
The business a Work was delivered for (e.g. Djampi Jawi, Ella Skin Care).
_Avoid_: Company, employer

**Deliverable**:
A single system delivered within a Work (e.g. "POS Kasir", "Panel Admin").
_Avoid_: App, sub-project

**Platform**:
A third-party technology or service RapiNexa can build on or integrate into a Service (e.g. OpenAI, AWS, Laravel, Midtrans). Shown as a logo only.
_Avoid_: Tech stack, skill, partner

**CTA**:
Any call-to-action on the site; every CTA opens a WhatsApp chat with RapiNexa, optionally pre-filled with the Service of interest.
_Avoid_: Contact form, hire me
